# Preregistration and ethics information

## Preregistration

Experiments 3 to 5 were preregistered on AsPredicted before data collection. The experiment numbers below follow the revised manuscript and the mapping in `experiment_renumbering.csv`.

| Revised experiment | Original project directory | AsPredicted record | Created | Public registration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exp3 | `study1` | #167984 | 26 March 2024 | https://aspredicted.org/TCQ_P59 |
| Exp4 | `study2` | #170042 | 9 April 2024 | https://aspredicted.org/D6C_1XS |
| Exp5 | `study3` | #172361 | 25 April 2024 | https://aspredicted.org/L66_KC6 |

No deviations from the preregistered protocols are reported for Exp3 to Exp5. Experiments 1, 2, and 6 were not preregistered.

## Ethics

All experimental procedures were approved by the Ethics Committee of the School of Psychology, Nanjing Normal University. All participants provided written informed consent before participation.

The reporting summary does not state an ethics approval number. This repository therefore does not invent one or include consent forms, signatures, internal correspondence, or other non-public institutional records.
