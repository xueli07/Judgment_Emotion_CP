# Stimulus availability

Facial stimuli derive from the Tsinghua Facial Expression Database:

Yang T, Yang Z, Xu G, et al. (2020). Tsinghua facial expression database: A database of facial expressions in Chinese young and older women and men. PLOS ONE 15(4): e0231304. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231304

The authors have permission to use the stimuli for the research and manuscript. Public redistribution permission for original faces, morphed faces, average faces, noise faces and face-derived classification images has not yet been confirmed. These images are therefore excluded from this release candidate.

Data and condition files retain stimulus filename labels as experimental metadata. Experiment 6 image reconstruction cannot run end to end without the restricted face and noise assets. Statistical analyses based on the released numerical tables remain available.
